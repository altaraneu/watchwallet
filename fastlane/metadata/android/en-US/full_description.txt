<p>WatchWallet shows what your crypto is worth and how it has done, all in one place, using <b>public addresses and extended public keys only</b>. It is open source and free, with no ads and no account.</p>
<p>This is an alpha version. Expect bugs, and do not use it as your only record of your funds. It is not an exchange or a custody service and offers no trading or investment advice.</p>
<p><b>Watch-only</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Cannot sign or send transactions.</li>
<li>Never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Input that looks like one is rejected and cleared, with a warning.</li>
<li>No check catches everything. A 32-byte Solana key looks the same as an address, and so does a key entered in the separate Sui and Aptos route. Never enter your keys in any app you do not fully trust, this one included.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Your portfolio</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Total value over time, allocation, and performance excluding deposits and withdrawals.</li>
<li>Cost basis and unrealized profit or loss, from your transaction history.</li>
<li>Charts for every coin and wallet, with live candlesticks from 1 minute to 1 week.</li>
<li>Transaction history, a price watchlist and a home screen widget.</li>
<li>Groups, for example one portfolio for each family member.</li>
<li>Prices in CZK, EUR, USD and more. 13 languages.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Adding wallets</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Paste or scan an address, xpub/ypub/zpub or output descriptor. The network is detected for you.</li>
<li>Import many at once from text or CSV.</li>
<li>Export to CSV, or to a JSON backup with optional encryption.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Supported networks</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Bitcoin Cash.</li>
<li>Ethereum and 20 other EVM networks, with tokens: Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche and more.</li>
<li>Solana, XRP, Tron, TON, Cardano, Stellar, Algorand, Tezos, Hedera, NEAR, Kaspa, Sui, Aptos.</li>
<li>Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Celestia, Injective, Polkadot, Kusama.</li>
<li>Not all of them are equally well tested in this alpha.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<ul>
<li>No developer servers, analytics, ads or Google Play Services. Wallets, labels and settings stay on your device, and cloud backup is off.</li>
<li>To load data, the app contacts public services directly. Blockchain services see the addresses you watch and your IP address. Price services see which coins you hold and your IP address.</li>
<li>Extended public keys stay on the device: addresses are derived locally. A server can still link addresses it is asked about together.</li>
<li>Optional Tor / SOCKS5 proxy and your own Bitcoin server, which hide your IP address or the addresses from public services.</li>
<li>App lock, discreet mode, and screenshots blocked by default.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Open source</b></p>
<p>The code, the full list of services and the limits of the app are public: github.com/altaraneu/watchwallet</p>
